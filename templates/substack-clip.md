HOW TO FILL THIS IN

title          Short, about the idea rather than the quote. "The word Trump left out",
               not "Migrants are making England unrecognisable" (that is his line, and as a
               headline it reads as your claim and makes the fight about the quote).

intro          Three or four sentences, first person, always opening "I was a guest on...".
               The three topics are the REAL ones from the whole segment, with specifics:
               figures, place names, dates. "the coming election in Saxony-Anhalt where the
               AfD polls near 40 percent", never "the German situation".
               Never a meta line pointing at the clip, such as "this is the part where I
               said X". The reader sees this before pressing play, so it has to be worth
               reading on its own.

summary        Two or three sentences of what the clip actually argues. Ends on the sharpest
               line, not on a conclusion that explains it away.

NO EM DASHES anywhere. Full stops, commas, or "and". They read as machine-written.
ENGLISH throughout, including the burned-in subtitles and the title card on the video
itself. The Croatian cut is a separate render for the Croatian channels.
